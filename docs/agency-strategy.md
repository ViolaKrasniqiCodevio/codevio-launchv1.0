## 1. Mission

> **We compress the distance between "we have an idea" and "we're live."**

Long form:

> Codevio is a two-to-three person studio of senior designers and engineers. We help early-stage founders in the EU and US look credible before launch or their next round — shipping brand, website, and working software in fixed sprints, weeks not quarters, without agency bloat, handoffs, or surprise invoices.

Short forms (for site/hero/footer):

- "From idea to launch, in weeks."
- "Built sharp. Built to last."

What the mission commits us to:

- We only take work we can ship inside a fixed sprint or a short series of them.
- Scope is locked before we start; changes become a new sprint, not an open-ended edit.
- Senior hands only. The people selling the work are the people doing the work.
- We measure ourselves on time-to-live, not billable hours.

## 2. Target market / niche

Lead with a **problem niche**, backed by a **vertical beachhead**:

| Layer | Definition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Problem niche | "Idea → launched" — brand, site, MVP | Repeatable, sellable with a timeline, fits a small senior team |
| Primary beachhead | Pre-seed / seed startups (SaaS, AI tools, fintech) in the EU/US | They buy fast, refer each other, and our stack (React/Next/Supabase) is directly relevant |
| Secondary | Creator / coach personal brands needing brand + funnel site | Fast decisions, pays upfront, one-off but good cash flow |
| Expand later | DTC / e-commerce launches, B2B SaaS marketing sites | Adjacent; use once proof exists |
| Avoid for now | Local service businesses (that is a *growth/results* positioning), enterprise, lowest-bidder work | Wrong buying process for a speed offer |

Where to start geographically: **UK / DACH / Nordics** first (time zone overlap and buying culture), US second.

## 3. Ideal client profile (ICP)

**Company**

- 1–15 employees, pre-seed to seed stage, or revenue-positive small brands
- EU / US based
- Has a launch or funding deadline driving urgency

**Buyer**

- Founder / CEO (decides in one call) or head of marketing
- No procurement, no committees
- Values communication and proof over credentials

**Trigger events**

- Raised a round, launching a product, rebrand, conference/demo day deadline, competitor just relaunched

**Budget**

- Landing Page Sprint €500 (entry) · Website Sprint €750 · MVP Sprint €1,000
- Launch Retainer €1,500–2,000/month (20 h capacity block)
- Scoping deep-dive €750, credited toward a sprint booked within 30 days
- Permanent list prices, published on the site

**Pains**

- Agencies quote 3 months and €50k
- Freelancers vanish mid-project
- No-code tools hit a ceiling right when things get serious
- Time-zone ghosting

**Where they are**

- LinkedIn (founder posts and DMs), X (build-in-public threads), startup Discords/Slacks
- Product Hunt recent launches (outbound within 30 days of a launch)
- Accelerator and founder networks (YC, EF, Seedcamp, local hubs), VC platform teams
- Overflow referrals from larger agencies that do not take small launch work

**Buying behavior**

- Wants a fixed price, a visible timeline, and a process — not an open retainer
- Decides based on trust signals: previous launches, speed metrics, clear communication

### ICP personas

| Persona | Situation | What they buy | Why they fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical founder, no designer** | MVP exists, brand/site do not; demo day or round coming | Website Sprint | Fastest sale; can judge engineering quality; refers other founders |
| **Non-technical founder, post-raise** | Budget exists, product does not; needs MVP + site | MVP Sprint (+ site) | Highest budget; needs more guidance; longer sales cycle |
| **Creator / coach / small brand** | Existing audience, weak funnel or dated brand | Website Sprint (one-off) | Fast decisions, pays upfront, good cash flow between startup sprints |

### Qualifying signals (first touch)

Green flags — the lead mentions:

- A concrete date ("demo day on the 30th", "launching before the conference")
- A recent raise or an active round
- Currently on Framer / Webflow / a template and hitting a ceiling
- The sender is the decision-maker
- Scope describable in one sentence

Red flags — escalate to the anti-ICP checklist below:

- "We'll figure out scope later"
- "We need it cheap, like this template"
- No date, no deadline, no urgency
- The sender is an intermediary with no authority

## 4. Anti-ICP (who we say no to)

- "Make it cheap, just like this template"
- Open-ended scope with no decision-maker in the room
- 40-revision feedback loops
- Projects below the sprint minimum, or "exposure" work
- Clients who need a full procurement process before a kickoff call
- Agencies looking to subcontract us as anonymous capacity (body-shop risk)
- Equity-only or revenue-share offers with no cash component
- Hype/crypto pump projects with brand risk and no real product

**Qualification checklist** (green = book a call, red = decline politely):

- [ ] Budget in sprint range
- [ ] Decision-maker on the call
- [ ] A real launch date or trigger event
- [ ] Scope describable in one sentence
- [ ] Values speed over endless polish loops

## 5. Positioning

> **For early-stage founders in the EU and US who need to look credible before launch or their next round, Codevio is the launch studio that ships brand, website, and MVP in fixed 2–4 week sprints — senior design and engineering, zero handoffs.**

Positioning pillars:

1. **Fixed sprints, not open retainers** — scope and timeline locked up front
2. **Senior-only team** — 2 makers, no juniors, no account managers
3. **One team end-to-end** — strategy, design, and engineering in the same room
4. **Async-first with EU/US overlap hours** — clear updates, no time-zone ghosting

Category vocabulary: **studio** (not "agency"). Keep this consistent everywhere.

Competitive frame:

| Against | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Freelancers | A team, a process, and a guaranteed ship date |
| Big agencies | Same senior output, a fraction of the time and overhead |
| No-code builders | Real engineering that does not hit a ceiling at launch |

**Objection to pre-arm:** "Fast means cheap." Answer: speed is a *process* claim, not a quality discount — fixed sprints, senior hands, and a public ship date.

**Second objection to pre-arm:** "A 2 person team might vanish mid-project." Answer: written scope, milestone payments (50/50), EU/US overlap hours, and 30 days of post-launch support — all in the agreement before kickoff.

**Outcome framing (recommended):** lead with what the buyer is actually buying — "look credible before launch or your next round." Speed is the mechanism; the funding/launch deadline is the motivation. This keeps urgency high and price sensitivity lower.

**AI-era differentiator:** AI builders (v0, Lovable, Bolt) commoditize "fast." Codevio's wedge is senior judgment and production engineering — brand direction, UX decisions, and code that does not hit a ceiling after launch. Message: AI-assisted speed, senior-grade output.

**Positioning tests to run:** keep the current "launch studio / fixed sprints" language for the next 10 leads; log which phrases prospects repeat back in calls (speed vs. credibility vs. senior-only). Rewrite the statement around whichever phrase recurs.

## 6. Productized offers

Canonical definitions — core services, full package specs, exclusions, add-ons, and package rules — live in [`services.md`](./services.md). Summary table:

| Offer | Promise | Timeline | Internal price | Core deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Landing Page Sprint (entry) | Idea → live landing page | 1 week | €500 | One high-converting page, copy shaping, analytics, deploy, 7-day support |
| Website Sprint | Idea → live website | 2–3 weeks | €750 | Brand direction, 5–7 page responsive site, copy support, analytics, launch checklist, 30-day support |
| MVP Sprint | Idea → working product | 4–6 weeks | €1,000 | Auth, payments, core flow, dashboard, deploy + handover, 30-day support |
| Launch Retainer | Keep shipping after launch | Monthly | €1,500–2,000/mo | Iteration capacity (20 h/month), new pages/features, performance + conversion tuning |

Rules:

- Every offer has a fixed scope, fixed timeline, and a named ship date.
- Prices are published on the site; "Request a quote" CTA routes to Contact for scope confirmation. Full list lives in `services.md`.
- Anything outside scope becomes the next sprint by default.
- Entry offer (Landing Page Sprint, €500) is the low-risk first purchase; its fee credits toward a Website or MVP Sprint booked within 30 days.
- Ship-date guarantee: if we miss the agreed date for reasons within our control, 10% back or a free retainer week.
